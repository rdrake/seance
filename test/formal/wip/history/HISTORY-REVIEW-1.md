## Fidelity

- **BatchAgrees is dispensable only with enough budget.** Contiguous pages and msgid anchors make every nonempty page move the anchor strictly backward; choosing a non-oldest first item repeats already fetched messages but skips no unseen older message. TimeOrdered also makes the floor stop safe. However, take held `{1}`, gap `{2,3,4}`, live `5`, all times equal, Limit=2, pages(2): reordered pages `[5,4]`, `[4,3]` stop on budget and miss 2; agreeing pages `[4,5]`, `[2,3]` recover the gap. Thus HNoBatchAgrees' five-page pass hides a budget-dependent loss, not a selection hole. Keep the positive, add this paired witness, and qualify its comment (`model/History.tla:96`, `:113`, `:148`, `:171`, `:176`; `model/mkhist.py:8-12`, `:27-29`; `DESIGN.md:80-89`).

- **Page semantics otherwise match the chosen restrictions.** LATEST `*`, strict timestamp BEFORE and positional msgid BEFORE are correct; ExactCount, nearest-selector selection and independent batch permutation implement D3/D5/D7/D10. Count deviations stop at Limit+1 by explicit decision, not by the protocol. There are no accidental selector guards in this path (`model/History.tla:89-114`; `DECISIONS.md:25-29`, `:101-104`, `:117-118`, `:200-205`; `../formal-spec/specs-src/extensions/chathistory.md:69`, `:83`, `:95`).

- **End generation is stronger than its wire meaning.** Requiring every eligible message in the page implements D22's explicit restriction, but is not generally equivalent to “next page empty” when batch order disagrees: the first item may still have predecessors already present in that page. The tag may safely stop recovery, yet the literal next query can be nonempty. Define truth against the actual next selector, or explicitly retain and name this abstraction; D22 only acknowledges the holey-page exception (`model/History.tla:114`, `:155`, `:176`; `DECISIONS.md:190-195`, `:424`; `../formal-spec/specs-src/extensions/chathistory.md:136`).

The query/receive split follows CH-23, including raw retrieved-message counting, whole-page overshoot, strict floor and first-item msgid anchoring; StopOnHeld and page budgets represent the described Seance policy. Merge dedupes by identity and maintains timestamp order from a sorted held buffer. No source-code refinement has happened (`model/History.tla:73-83`, `:120-130`, `:136-179`; `DECISIONS.md:158-164`, `:248-258`, `:421-423`; `DESIGN.md:27`, `:119`, `:187-192`; `../formal-spec/specs-src/extensions/chathistory.md:192-208`).
